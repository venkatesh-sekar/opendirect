Review pictures and clips at full size. On the canvas, double-click a generated output or a media node (or use the magnifier in the node header) to open a viewer with zoom, pan and Open / Reveal in folder; the arrow keys step through every output of the run, each captioned with its model, prompt and size, without changing which take is picked. On a container's Assets tab, clicking a picture or clip opens the same viewer and steps through the grid as filtered.
